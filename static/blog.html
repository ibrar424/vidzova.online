Format Guide

MP4 vs MP3 vs WebM: Which Format Should You Choose?

Understanding which file format suits your goal saves storage and ensures seamless playback across all your devices:

  • MP4 (H.264): The gold standard for universal video compatibility. Plays natively on Apple, Android, Windows, Smart TVs, and video editing suites (Premiere, Final Cut, CapCut).
  • MP3 (320kbps / 192kbps): Ideal for podcasts, audiobooks, lectures, and background music. Extreme storage efficiency with audio-only playback on any media player.
  • WebM: High compression format developed by Google for HTML5 web streaming. Great for lightweight browser playback, but less universally supported by older offline media players.

Security & Privacy

How to Avoid Fake Video Downloader Sites & Notification Adware

Many legacy video downloaders monetize through aggressive advertising techniques that can compromise your device security. Learn what red flags to watch for:

Key safety tips:
  • Never allow notification prompts: Shady sites ask "Click Allow to verify you are not a robot" or "Allow notifications to download". These register adware pushes directly to your desktop or mobile lockscreen.
  • Never install required browser extensions: True video downloading happens server-side; any site requiring an extension to save standard MP4 files is likely bundle-installing affiliate trackers.
  • Look for clean, transparent utilities: Trustworthy tools like Vidzova operate with zero pop-up tabs, no notification prompts, and clear DMCA copyright policies.
