Copyright & DMCA Policy

Digital Millennium Copyright Act (17 U.S.C. § 512) Compliance & Takedown Guidelines

⚠️ Crucial Usage Notice: Vidzova is designed exclusively for personal, educational, and backup use. Only download content you own or have explicit permission to save. Respect copyright and each platform’s terms of service. Vidzova does not support or condone the unauthorized reproduction, redistribution, or commercial use of copyrighted materials.

Vidzova respects the intellectual property rights of artists, publishers, creators, and third-party platforms. It is our policy to respond expeditiously to clear, valid notices of alleged copyright infringement that comply with the Digital Millennium Copyright Act of 1998 (17 U.S.C. § 512, "DMCA") and international intellectual property regulations.

1. Technical Nature of Our Service

Vidzova operates purely as a technical tool that accesses publicly available media content at the explicit direction of the user. Vidzova:

  • Does not host, store, stream, or index video or audio files on our servers.
  • Does not maintain a database of copyrighted content or facilitate piracy.
  • Transmits media data directly from public source servers to the end user's client browser.
  • Strictly blocks access to DRM-protected and subscription-gated platforms (including Netflix, Spotify, Hulu, Disney+, Amazon Prime Video, etc.).

2. Procedure for Filing a DMCA Notice

If you are a copyright owner or an agent authorized to act on behalf of one, and you believe that content processed through our platform infringes your copyright, please submit a written notification including all of the following details:

  1. Identification of the Copyrighted Work: A description or link to the specific copyrighted work you claim has been infringed.
  2. Specific URL(s): The exact URL(s) on the supported platforms that host the infringing content.
  3. Contact Information: Your full legal name, company or organization, physical mailing address, telephone number, and valid email address.
  4. Good Faith Statement: A statement that you have a good faith belief that the disputed use of the material is not authorized by the copyright owner, its agent, or the law.
  5. Accuracy & Perjury Statement: A statement, made under penalty of perjury, that the information provided in your notice is accurate and that you are the copyright owner or authorized to act on the owner's behalf.
  6. Signature: A physical or electronic signature of the copyright owner or authorized representative.

3. Repeat Infringer Policy

In accordance with Section 512(i)(1)(A) of the DMCA, Vidzova implements technical measures to block specific URLs, domains, and IP addresses associated with repeated or willful infringement upon verified notice.

4. Counter-Notification Procedure

If you believe that your content was blocked or removed by mistake or misidentification, you may submit a written counter-notification to our Designated Agent. The counter-notice must contain:

  • Identification of the material that was removed or disabled.
  • Your name, address, telephone number, and email.
  • A statement under penalty of perjury that you have a good faith belief the material was removed by mistake or misidentification.
  • Your consent to the jurisdiction of the federal court for your judicial district.
  • Your physical or electronic signature.

5. Prohibition of Circumvention

Vidzova does not bypass or circumvent digital rights management (DRM) or access-control technologies. Any request attempting to process protected or encrypted streams will fail automatically and be rejected by our extraction system.
