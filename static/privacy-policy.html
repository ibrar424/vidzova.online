Legal & Privacy

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 26, 2026 • Vidzova Platform

At Vidzova (accessible via https://vidzova.online), protecting the privacy and personal data of our users is an absolute cornerstone of our service design. This Privacy Policy outlines the standards and practices governing user privacy across our website.

Zero Video Storage

We do not store, catalog, or archive media files. Download processing occurs ephemerally in volatile memory.

No Personal Accounts

Vidzova is 100% free with no mandatory account creation, password requirements, or personal identity collection.

Encrypted Sessions

Every transaction and request is secured with modern SSL/TLS HTTPS encryption from end to end.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

Vidzova operates as a stateless client-directed media proxy tool. As such, our systems never gather or retain:

  • Downloaded Videos or Audios: We do not keep copies of media fetched through our platform. Once streamed to your device, no record remains on our infrastructure.
  • Personal Identifiable Information (PII): We do not request, store, or sell names, email addresses, phone numbers, or billing data.
  • Search or Download Histories: Queries are processed on-demand and are not linked to user accounts or tracking profiles.

2. Data Collected for Operational Security

To prevent automated bot abuse, DDOS attacks, and service degradation, our web servers temporarily record standard non-identifying technical metadata:

  • Browser user-agent, operating system, and interface language.
  • IP addresses (used exclusively for real-time rate limiting and security filtering).
  • Anonymized HTTP request status codes and response latency diagnostics.

3. Browser Cookies & Local Preferences

Vidzova does not require tracking cookies. We utilize standard browser localStorage purely to remember your chosen visual theme (Dark Mode / Light Mode) across visits. You can delete or disable local storage in your browser settings at any time without impacting downloader functionality.

4. Third-Party Advertising & Analytics

To sustain free server operations, Vidzova may display third-party advertisements or use aggregated analytics (such as Google Analytics). These providers may use cookies and web beacons to serve advertisements based on web traffic. You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ad Settings or the Digital Advertising Alliance consumer choice page.

5. GDPR and CCPA Rights

Under the European Union General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), individuals possess rights regarding access to, deletion of, and portability of their personal data. Because Vidzova does not collect or retain user personal data, we store zero user dossiers or personally identifiable records.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions, clarifications, or requests concerning this Privacy Policy, please email our privacy team at support@vidzova.online.
